Privacy

Your supply-chain information should stay yours.

This page explains, in plain language, how RouteTwin plans to handle business information supplied for an introductory conversation, a pilot, or a demonstration.

Last updated: 28 September 2026 · Early-stage draft

Our commitment

Clear purpose.
Careful handling.

RouteTwin uses information only to discuss, map, test, and report on the work agreed with a client.

Ask a privacy question →
01

What information may be collected

RouteTwin may collect business contact details and the information a business chooses to provide about its supply chain. This could include locations, suppliers, products, routes, stock cover, disruption concerns, and approved backup options.

For a demonstration, please use fictional or approved sample information. RouteTwin does not need passwords, banking details, personal customer records, or information unrelated to the agreed work.

02

How information is used

Information is used to prepare the RouteTwin work agreed with the client. This may include mapping a supply chain, testing disruption scenarios, comparing possible responses, and preparing a discussion or report.

RouteTwin does not sell client information or use it for unrelated marketing.

03

Who information is shared with

RouteTwin will not share a client’s information with another business unless the client has given permission, sharing is needed for an agreed service, or the law requires it.

Any future use of specialist software or service providers will be discussed with the client where it affects their information.

04

How information is handled

RouteTwin aims to keep information limited to what is needed, use sensible access controls, and avoid keeping it longer than necessary for the agreed work.

The current client portal is a local demonstration only: an uploaded workbook is read in the browser on that device and is not sent to a RouteTwin server. It is not yet a secure client-login system.

05

Your choices

A client may ask what business information RouteTwin holds, ask for a correction, or ask for information to be deleted when it is no longer needed. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or an agreed contract.

06

Contact RouteTwin

For a privacy question, correction request, or concern, email routetwin.business@gmail.com. RouteTwin will aim to respond clearly and promptly.

This is an early-stage, plain-English privacy page. Before RouteTwin accepts real client data at scale, this page should be reviewed and updated with professional Australian privacy advice.
