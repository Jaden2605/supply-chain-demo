Why RouteTwin

Better decisions when the normal route stops working.

RouteTwin gives a team one simple visual model of suppliers, ports, factories, warehouses, retailers, and backup choices. It is built for businesses that need to understand disruption before it becomes a bigger problem.

A useful answer to the questions that matter most

01

What is affected?

See the locations, routes, and stock buffers that may be affected by a disruption.

02

What can we do?

Compare alternative suppliers, ports, transport routes, and delivery plans.

03

What is the trade-off?

Compare estimated time, cost, and risk before choosing a response.

A practical first use

For Australian manufacturers with global supply chains

The prototype demonstrates a company using suppliers in Taiwan and Vietnam, a Singapore backup port, an Australian factory, a Netherlands distribution hub, and customers across Europe and North America.
